Step 01

Get StudyCap from the Chrome Web Store

StudyCap runs as a Chrome extension. Install it once and it is ready on every Panopto lecture tab.

  • Open the official Chrome Web Store listing.
  • Press Add to Chrome → Add extension.
  • Pin StudyCap so it’s always in your toolbar.
Add StudyCap to Chrome
Step 02

Sign in with your StudyCap account

Use the same login you’d use on the web app. If you haven’t created an account yet, it only takes a few seconds.

  • Click Sign in in the extension popup.
  • Choose Google to authenticate securely.
  • Need an account? Use the Sign Up button first.
Sign in or create account
Step 03

Open a Panopto lecture & launch StudyCap

With the extension installed and signed in, open the Panopto recording you want to summarize and click the StudyCap icon.

  • Navigate to Panopto and play any lecture.
  • Click the StudyCap icon, then click Generate Summary.
  • Watch the AI summary flow straight into your dashboard.
