When you use StudyCap to generate summaries or quizzes, the AI processes the text you provide solely for the duration of the session. We do not keep or archive your content.

Cookie Policy

Our Cookie Approach

StudyCap only uses essential cookies for authentication and core functionality. However, when you use our app, third-party services may set non-essential cookies that we cannot control.

Essential Cookies (Set by StudyCap)

We only set cookies that are absolutely necessary for our app to function:

  • Session Cookies: To maintain your login status and authentication
  • Security Cookies: To protect your account and prevent unauthorized access
  • Preference Cookies: To remember your basic app settings

Third-Party Services That May Set Cookies

When you use StudyCap, these external services may set additional cookies on your device:

  • Google OAuth: For user authentication (may include analytics and tracking cookies)
  • Google Fonts: For typography display (may include tracking cookies)
  • Stripe: For payment processing (may include analytics and fraud prevention cookies)
  • External CDNs: For loading JavaScript libraries and CSS (may include performance cookies)

Important Notice

We cannot control what cookies these third-party services set. These external services are necessary for our app to function properly, and they may set their own cookies for analytics, tracking, or other purposes.

If you don't like cookies being set by these third-party services, please don't use our app.

Your Choice

By continuing to use StudyCap, you acknowledge that these third-party services may set cookies on your device. We cannot disable these cookies without breaking our app's functionality.

If you're concerned about third-party cookies, you can:

  • Use browser settings to block third-party cookies
  • Use privacy-focused browsers or extensions
  • Choose not to use our app if you don't want these cookies

Browser Cookie Management

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can usually find these settings in the "Options" or "Preferences" menu of your browser. You can delete existing cookies, allow or block all cookies, or block cookies from particular sites.
