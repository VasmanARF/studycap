Get Started With StudyCap

1

Get StudyCap from the Chrome Web Store

StudyCap is available as a Chrome extension. Click the button below to open the Chrome Web Store page in a new tab and add StudyCap to your browser.

Add StudyCap to browser
2

Sign in to StudyCap

Once you've installed the extension, you'll need to sign in to your StudyCap account. If you don't have an account yet, you can create one on our login page.

Sign Up
3

Open the StudyCap Extension

After signing in, you can access StudyCap by clicking on the extension icon in your browser's toolbar. You must have the Panopto lecture recording open in your browser to use the extension.

Congratulations!

You're all set to use StudyCap! Start transforming your lectures into interactive study materials, summaries, and more.
