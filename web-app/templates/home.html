Transform Lengthy Lectures Into Concise Summaries

Upload your lecture recordings or notes, and StudyCap will analyze the content to produce comprehensive summaries highlighting the key points, concepts, and examples. Our advanced AI understands academic content across various disciplines, from science and mathematics to humanities and social sciences.

Example: A 2-hour lecture on Quantum Mechanics is transformed into a clear, structured 2-page summary with all essential formulas, principles, and experimental evidence highlighted for easy revision.

Get Instant Answers to Your Questions

Confused about a concept? Simply ask StudyCap any question related to your lecture material. Our AI will provide detailed, accurate answers tailored to your learning context. Think of it as having a dedicated tutor available 24/7.

Example: When asked "Explain the difference between mitosis and meiosis in simple terms," StudyCap provides a clear comparison table highlighting key differences, along with visual explanations and memorable analogies to help you understand and remember the concepts.

Visualize Complex Concepts with AI-Generated Mind Maps

Our Premium plan includes automatic generation of visual mind maps that connect related concepts in your lectures. This visual approach helps consolidate knowledge and understand relationships between different topics.

Example: A lecture series on World War II is transformed into an interactive mind map showing causal relationships between events, key figures, political movements, and economic factors, helping you grasp the broader historical context.

Reinforce Your Learning with Custom Quizzes

StudyCap generates personalized quizzes based on your lecture content. These quizzes adapt to your knowledge level, focusing more on areas where you need additional practice.

Example: After studying organic chemistry reactions, StudyCap creates a progressive quiz that starts with basic reaction identification and gradually challenges you with predicting products and mechanisms, strengthening your understanding step by step.

Build Your Personal Knowledge Repository

All your lecture summaries, notes, and generated content are organized in a searchable database. Easily find and review information across all your courses when preparing for exams or working on assignments.

Example: When preparing for your final exams, quickly access all relevant content across multiple courses by searching for key terms like "neural networks" or "climate models," pulling information from different lectures where these concepts appeared.
